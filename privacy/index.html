Privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Ambicar collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no internet connections of its own.

Bluetooth

The iPhone and Apple Watch apps use Bluetooth only to talk to the Ambicar controller in your car: to read the car's state (doors, lights, ignition, lock) and to send light settings. This data stays between your devices and the controller.

Data on your device

The app keeps your saved colours and an event log on your device. The log records what the app and the car did (for example "connected", "door opened") and never leaves the device unless you tap Share in Settings → Event log and choose where to send it. Settings → Event log → Clear deletes it. Deleting the app removes everything.

Location

Ambicar does not use your location. iOS may show Bluetooth as location-related; the app never reads GPS.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: GitHub Issues.
