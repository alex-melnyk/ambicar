Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, open an issue and you'll get a reply there.

Connecting

The app can't find my car

The controller sleeps with the car. Unlock the car or switch the ignition on, keep the phone within a few metres, and make sure Bluetooth is on and Ambicar is allowed to use it (Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth).

It says the controller rejected the app

Each controller is paired with its own app key. Use the app build that came with your controller.

How many phones can connect?

Up to three devices at once (for example, two phones and an Apple Watch). Changes made on one show on the others.

Light

How do I change the colour?

Colour tab: drag the ring or tap a preset. The bar at the bottom of the Car tab sets brightness; tap its colour to jump to the Colour tab.

Can each event have its own colour?

Yes. Events tab → tap an event → Colour. Ignition on and off use the driving colour; Goodbye (lock) only fades out what is lit.

Preview doesn't light the doors

Previews need the car awake and the door boards online (Settings → Door boards). After a preview the doors go back to the car's real state.

Apple Watch and CarPlay

Watch

The watch app connects to the controller on its own: turn the Digital Crown for brightness, swipe for colour presets.

CarPlay

One screen with colour presets and brightness buttons, available once CarPlay support is approved by Apple.

Demo

Can I try the app without a controller?

Yes. On the first screen tap "No controller yet? Try the demo", or Settings → Demo car → Start demo. Settings → Exit demo goes back.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question on GitHub Issues. Settings → Event log → Share attaches a log that helps find the cause; it contains only what the app and the car did.
