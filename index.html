Your car's ambient light,
in your pocket

Ambicar controls the door ambient lighting of an Audi Q7 4L fitted with the Ambicar controller. Pick a colour, set the brightness and choose how the doors greet you.

Get helpPrivacy policy

What it does

Colour and brightness

A colour ring and 16 presets for the driving colour, with brightness from 0 to 100 %.

Events

Unlock, door open, ignition, indicators, hazard, tailgate, exit light and lock, each on or off with its own colour.

Preview on the car

Play any event on the doors from the app to see how it looks.

Live car

Doors, lights, ignition and lock state, drawn on the car as it happens.

Apple Watch and CarPlay

Brightness and colour presets on your wrist and on the car's screen.

Demo

No controller yet? Try every screen with a demo car from the first launch.

On your wrist

The watch app talks to the car on its own. Turn the Digital Crown for brightness, swipe for colours.

How it works

The Ambicar controller replaces the car's original ambient light module. It listens to the car and drives the door light strips. The app talks to the controller over Bluetooth only: no account, no internet, nothing leaves your phone.
